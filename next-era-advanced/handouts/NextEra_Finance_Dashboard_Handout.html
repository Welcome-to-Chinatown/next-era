A dashboard to fix your finances

A small home-goods shop has 100 orders in a messy spreadsheet. Ask AI for a dashboard that shows what is selling, what is returned, and when.

The sample file

Home-goods shop, 100 orders. Messy on purpose: mixed date styles, category names that do not match, extra spaces, refunds in their own column. Sample data, not a real business.

Download retail_store_data.csv

1Run it plain

Attach the file to a new chat, then send this. Note what is vague.

Here is my shop's sales file. Make me a dashboard.

2Rebuild it with four parts

Fill in the [brackets], attach the file, and run it in a new chat.

Role & goal: You are my business analyst. I run [type of shop] and I want a dashboard that shows how my shop is doing.
Steps:
1. Clean the data: read all the date styles, merge category names that mean the same thing, trim extra spaces.
2. Calculate total revenue (use the Total column), number of orders, average order value, and total refunds. Do not subtract refunds from revenue.
3. Show sales by category and sales by month.
4. List the top 10 items by revenue.
Output: A simple dashboard with four number cards, a bar chart, a line chart, and a table. Plain English labels, no jargon.
Eval: Before you answer, check your totals against the file and tell me which cleaning choices you made.
