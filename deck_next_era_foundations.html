Section one

What is AI

The core concept

What is an LLM

Large Language Model
A program trained on a massive amount of human language, trillions of words, most of the public internet and more. That training is why it reads and writes the way people do, and predicts the next word so well.
The name is the definition: Large is the huge training data, Language is human writing, Model predicts the next word. Every chat assistant you know, ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, is one.
Ground the room

What AI is, and is not

It is
  • Predictive. It guesses the most likely next word, based on patterns it has seen.
  • A fast drafter. Quick, tireless, confident. You decide what ships.
  • A tool that reads and writes. Newer tools also create documents, images, even spreadsheets.
It is not
  • Magic. It does not understand. It pattern-matches, and it can be confidently wrong.
  • Aware of your business unless you tell it.
  • A replacement for your judgment.
Good to know

The vocabulary of AI

Prompt
Your instruction to the AI. Prompt engineering is shaping that instruction to get a good answer.
Context window
The AI's working memory for one conversation. Your prompt, any files, and its reply all share the same space.
Tokens
The pieces of text the AI counts, roughly a word each. More tokens, more it costs.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
Give the AI your own documents to answer from. Example: upload your menu or policies to NotebookLM and ask it questions. Claude Projects and custom GPTs work the same way. No coding.
Agentic AI
AI that takes actions, browses, fills forms, not just answers.
Open source
Models like Llama, DeepSeek, Qwen, or Kimi K2 that you can run on your own device. Private, but technical to set up.

The BuiltSmall framework

How to find where AI fits in your business. Find the task that drains your week, check that it fits AI, then ask it well.

The method

Start with the task that drains your week

Start with the work you would hand off in a second if you could.

The repeating, draining tasks that eat your time.

For example
  • Reconciling sales
  • Sorting receipts
  • Reordering stock
  • Writing the same caption again
Call it out

What is your PITA? The pain in the ass task.

We will test a few on the next slide.

Is it a job for AI

Three checks

1
Procedure
Can you write down the steps?
Yes: sorting receiptsNo: choosing a business partner
2
Repetition
Does it repeat in the same shape?
Yes: a weekly reorder emailNo: a one-time legal fight
3
Risk
Low risk, let AI run. High risk, AI drafts and you check.
Low: a draft captionHigh: filing your taxes
Passes all three? That is where you start.
The PITA: procurement

Procurement

One list, a dashboard, and every email

One inventory file becomes a reorder dashboard and a stack of supplier emails.

  • The pain: knowing what is low, what it costs to restock, and emailing every supplier by hand.
  • One prompt, four parts: role and goal, steps, output, eval.
  • What it makes: a reorder dashboard — items below par, reorder cost by supplier, a quick chart — plus one ready-to-send email per supplier.
  • The catch: it works off the numbers you give it. Confirm quantities before you send.

Lead with the human

Safety and privacy

01
Keep a human in the loop. AI drafts, you decide. Never let it send money, sign, hire, or post on its own.
02
Keep private things out. No card or account numbers, passwords, SSNs, or customer and employee records in public AI tools.
03
Assume free tools may learn from what you type. Turn off training in settings, or use a business plan for sensitive work.
04
AI makes things up. Verify names, numbers, dates, and anything legal, tax, or medical with a person.
05
Start inside your business, not pointed at customers. A public chatbot can be wrong in public. Inside tools are easier to check.
New scams use AI voice cloning and better phishing. Verify any unusual money or access request by phone.
Protect your data

Turn off training

Most chat assistants can stop training on what you type. Here is where to find the setting.

ChatGPT
Settings › Data controls › turn off "Improve the model for everyone".
Claude
Settings › Privacy › turn off "Help improve our AI models".
Gemini
Settings › Gemini Apps Activity › turn it Off.

The core principle

Using AI effectively

Vague ask
"Write me a caption."
A generic caption that could belong to any business.
Specific ask
Role & goal: You are my social media manager. Write an Instagram caption for my tea shop's new jasmine milk tea.
Steps: Lead with the flavor, say it is new this week, end with a call to visit.
Output: Friendly tone, under 30 words, in English and Chinese.
Eval: Check it reads naturally in both languages.
A caption you can actually post.
The quality of your input sets the quality of your output.
How to ask

The prompt framework

PartWhat to doExample
Role and GoalTell AI who it is and what to accomplishYou are my bookkeeper. Sort my expenses for last month.
StepsBreak the task into clear numbered stepsStep 1, group by category. Step 2, total each one. Step 3, flag anything unusual.
OutputSay exactly what you want backGive me a table I can paste into a spreadsheet.
EvalAsk AI to check its own workConfirm every line is counted once and the totals add up.
